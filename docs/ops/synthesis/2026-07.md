This synthesis is the direct proof: the July 26 heartbeat predicted it would land 2026-08-03 ("first Monday of August"); per the corrected schedule it lands 2026-08-01, as it should.

The bet was honored, not just stated.

## What Didn't Work?

### Failed Experiments

- **#52 crossed its own trigger and the on-ramp still didn't move.** The June synthesis resized #52 to a first-step-only bet ("collate the pilot data into one doc"). It didn't move in any of July's four heartbeats — a ninth consecutive week of zero movement. The 07-13 heartbeat set an explicit rule: a third consecutive non-movement heartbeat would force this synthesis to reconsider the on-ramp's shape rather than re-ask again. The 07-20 heartbeat confirmed the trigger fired. See the decision below — repeating the ask a fifth month running is no longer the right move.
- **Zero product or content work landed for the entire month.** All 8 merges were heartbeat/synthesis/schedule meta-PRs. Excluding those, "no product work" now extends across the back half of June and all of July.

### Lessons Learned

- **Resizing a task doesn't create capacity.** June's bet correctly diagnosed #52 as over-sized for the available time and shrank it to a single-doc on-ramp. That was the right move to test, and it's now been tested for a full month with no result — the honest read is that the constraint is capacity, not task shape. A further resize would just be repeating the same experiment a third time.
- **A WIP-1 item that hasn't moved in two months is a hygiene question, not just a patience question.** #52 has occupied the sole active "Now" slot since late May without violating the WIP=2 limit, but it also hasn't been "in progress" in any real sense for most of that time. The board technically stayed within policy while quietly describing a parked item as active work.
